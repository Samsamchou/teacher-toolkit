## Mission 1｜國際來賓資料站 Guest Data Station

**Mission English：**Three guest cards lost their country data. Read each arrival clue. Restore the first three records.<br>
**Mission 難字：**guest｜來賓；country data｜國家資料；clue｜線索；restore｜修復；record｜紀錄。<br>
**beforeState：**二水車站虛構接待台顯示 `0/3 RECORDS RESTORED`。<br>
**Mission reward：**三題完成後取得 Arrival Data Chip，啟動人物身分掃描器。

### U01-M1-Q1｜Maya 的空白國籍欄

- **來源／目標：**U01 核心 1；Australia；L1 Recognize；選單字。
- **Mission English：**Maya’s guest card is blank. Read her badge and save the answer.
- **難字表：**guest card｜來賓資料卡；blank｜空白的；badge｜名牌；save｜儲存。
- **證據：**虛構資料卡顯示 `Name: Maya`、`Country: Australia`；接待員直接問 Maya：`Where are you from?`
- **情境問題：**Complete Maya’s answer: `I’m from ____.`
- **選項：**A. Australia　B. India　C. Japan
- **正解：**A. Australia。
- **干擾項檢核：**India、Japan 都是同任務中會出現的真實國名，但與 Maya 的 Country 欄衝突；選項長度與樣式一致。
- **第一次提示：**Look at the Country line on Maya’s badge.
- **第二次提示：**Maya is speaking. Complete `I’m from ...` with her country.
- **答對解析：**The badge says Australia. Maya speaks about herself, so the full answer is `I’m from Australia.`
- **故事狀態：**Maya 的空白欄恢復為 Australia；進度變成 `1/3`，取得 `Arrival Fragment A`。
- **SEL／重試：**先查看欄位再作答；錯答只要求回看 Country，不羞辱、不阻斷。

### U01-M1-Q2｜Andy 的來源答案

- **來源／目標：**U01 核心 2；India；L2 Apply；選完整答句。
- **Mission English：**Andy’s country line is missing. Use his tag to complete the record.
- **難字表：**country line｜國家欄；missing｜遺失的；tag｜標籤；complete｜補完整。
- **證據：**虛構資料卡顯示 `Name: Andy`、男孩圖示、行李標籤 `India`；控制台顯示 `Where is Andy from?`
- **情境問題：**Which answer repairs Andy’s record?
- **選項：**A. She is from India.　B. He is from India.　C. He is from the USA.
- **正解：**B. He is from India.
- **干擾項檢核：**A 國家相符但代名詞與人物衝突；C 代名詞相符但國家與標籤衝突。
- **第一次提示：**Check Andy’s picture and country tag.
- **第二次提示：**Andy is `he`. His tag says India.
- **答對解析：**Andy is a boy, so use `he`. His tag says India. The answer is `He is from India.`
- **故事狀態：**Andy 的人物與 India 標籤連結；進度 `2/3`，取得 `Arrival Fragment B`。
- **SEL／重試：**同時核對人物與國家，不只抓一個單字猜答案。

### U01-M1-Q3｜Ken 的衝突掃描（Power Question）

- **來源／目標：**U01 延伸；Japan；L3 Integrate；選完整答句。
- **Mission English：**Ken’s scan has three clues. Find the wrong country and repair his answer.
- **難字表：**scan｜掃描資料；wrong｜錯誤的；repair｜修正；three clues｜三條線索。
- **證據：**`Name: Ken`、男孩圖示、正確國家卡 `Japan`；故障掃描器問 `Is he from Korea?`
- **情境問題：**Which answer matches the person, the question, and the country card?
- **選項：**A. Yes, he is.　B. No, she isn’t. She’s from Japan.　C. No, he isn’t. He’s from Japan.
- **正解：**C. No, he isn’t. He’s from Japan.
- **干擾項檢核：**A 忽略 Korea 與 Japan 的衝突；B 國家正確但代名詞與 Ken 衝突。
- **第一次提示：**Compare Korea in the question with the country card.
- **第二次提示：**The countries do not match. Ken is `he`.
- **答對解析：**The scan asks about Korea, but Ken’s card says Japan. Say `No, he isn’t. He’s from Japan.`
- **故事狀態：**第三張資料修復，`3/3`；三塊 Fragment 組成 Arrival Data Chip，解鎖 Mission 2。
- **SEL／重試：**把人物、問句與國家三條線索一起驗證，培養修正判斷。

### U01-M2-Q1｜Amy 遺失的問句

- **來源／目標：**U01 核心 3；Spain；L1→L2；選完整問句。
- **Mission English：**Amy’s answer is saved, but her question is gone. Restore her missing dialogue.
- **難字表：**saved｜已儲存的；gone｜不見的；restore｜還原；dialogue｜對話。
- **證據：**虛構檔案顯示 `Name: Amy`、女孩圖示、`Country: Spain`；已儲存答句 `She is from Spain.`
- **情境問題：**Which question completes Amy’s dialogue?
- **選項：**A. `Where is Amy from?`　B. `Where is Andy from?`　C. `Is Amy from India?`
- **正解：**A. `Where is Amy from?`
- **干擾項檢核：**B 問錯人物；C 是 Yes／No 問句，無法自然配對現有的開放式答句。
- **第一次提示：**The saved answer is about Amy and asks for a country.
- **第二次提示：**Use `Where is ... from?` with Amy’s name.
- **答對解析：**`Where is Amy from?` matches `She is from Spain.` The question asks for her country.
- **故事狀態：**Amy 的問答回路恢復，掃描器亮起 `1/3`，取得 `Lens Part A`。
- **SEL／重試：**從答句反推問句，驗證人物與溝通功能。

### U01-M2-Q2｜Sofia 的錯誤國家

- **來源／目標：**U01 核心 4；the USA；L2 Apply；選完整答句。
- **Mission English：**The scanner shows India, but Sofia’s card shows another country. Correct the wrong reply.
- **難字表：**shows｜顯示；another｜另一個；correct｜改正；reply｜答句。
- **證據：**虛構檔案顯示 Sofia、女孩圖示、`Country: the USA`；掃描器問 `Is she from India?`
- **情境問題：**Which reply corrects the scanner?
- **選項：**A. `Yes, she is.`　B. `No, she isn’t. She’s from the USA.`　C. `No, she isn’t. She’s from the UK.`
- **正解：**B. `No, she isn’t. She’s from the USA.`
- **干擾項檢核：**A 忽略兩國衝突；C 否定形式正確，但修正後國家與 Sofia 的卡片衝突。
- **第一次提示：**Compare India with the Country line.
- **第二次提示：**They do not match. Keep `the` before USA.
- **答對解析：**India and the USA do not match. Say `No, she isn’t. She’s from the USA.`
- **故事狀態：**Sofia 從錯誤國家隊伍移回正確檔案；掃描器 `2/3`，取得 `Lens Part B`。
- **SEL／重試：**發現錯誤後用完整英文修正，不只按 No。

### U01-M2-Q3｜Emma 的雙層核對（Power Question）

- **來源／目標：**U01 延伸；the UK；L3 Integrate；選完整答句。

- **正解：**C. Yes, she is.
- **干擾項檢核：**A 國家判斷可能正確但代名詞錯；B 與人物卡的國家衝突。
- **第一次提示：**Check the selected person and the country screen.
- **第二次提示：**Emma is `she`. Both screens show the UK.
- **答對解析：**Emma uses `she`, and her card matches the UK. The answer is `Yes, she is.`
- **故事狀態：**Identity Lens 完成，`IDENTITY MATCH: ON`；掃描器投射團隊人數，解鎖 Mission 3。
