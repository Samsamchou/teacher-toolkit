# Monster in My Mind v1.0

## Project baseline / 專案基線

- 我是國小英語老師 / I am an elementary English teacher.
- 所有檔案用繁體中文以及英文 / All project files use Traditional Chinese and English.
- 教學對象是國小 3–6 年級學生 / The teaching audience is Grade 3–6 elementary students.

## Project goal / 專案目標

Build a privacy-conscious, responsive English × SEL × Art × AI classroom tool for Taiwanese Grade 5–6 students. Students complete a supported SEL sentence, design a monster, choose an art style and background, and receive a fixed 16:9 image prompt without an AI image API, login, or personal-data storage.

建立適合臺灣國小五、六年級的 English × SEL × Art × AI 互動工具。學生完成句型鷹架、設計 Monster、選擇畫風與場景，最後取得固定 16:9 的圖像提示詞；第一版不使用圖像 API、不要求登入，也不儲存個資。

## Content and safety / 內容與安全

- Keep all existing sibling tools in `tools/` untouched.
- Keep all phrase, emotion, monster, style, and background content in typed local data.
- Do not ask for student names, rank worries, score emotions, publish work, or store personal data.
- Keep UI copy bilingual, English-first with Traditional Chinese support.
