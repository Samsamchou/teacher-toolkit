② 感恩節小知識（選 1 句）

  1. People have turkey on Thanksgiving Day.人們在感恩節吃火雞。
  2. The Pilgrims came to America on the Mayflower.清教徒搭五月花號來到美洲。
  3. The Pilgrims had a harvest celebration in 1621.清教徒在 1621 年舉辦豐收慶祝。
  4. Families have a big feast together.家人一起享用豐盛大餐。
  5. People eat pumpkin pie for dessert.人們吃南瓜派當甜點。
  6. On Thanksgiving, people say what they are thankful for.感恩節時，人們說出自己感謝的事。
