# HWG5 U01–U02 RPG 題庫（沿用原本的拼字＋拖放題）

共 32 題，每題 3.125 分，總分 100（四捨五入到整數）。題目圖片沿用闖關任務。
拼字題：字母方塊（含多給的干擾字母）拼出單字；拖放題：把字卡拖進空格（含干擾字卡）。第一次送出就全對才算分。

| 題 | 題型 | 題目（____ 是空格） | 正解 | 干擾 | 答錯時的解題秘笈 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | 拖放 | Tick: What ____ is it?<br>Ken: It's ____. | **time / 9:50** | 多給的字卡：day、9:15、5:50 | 問時間用 What time is it?。短針靠近 10、長針指著 10，所以是 9:50。 |
| Q02 | 拖放 | Tick: What day ____ it?<br>Ken: It's ____. | **is / Monday** | 多給的字卡：do、are、Sunday | 問星期幾用 What day is it?，it 後面配 is。圖上亮的是 Monday（星期一）。 |
| Q03 | 拖放 | Tick: Is it ____ today?<br>Ken: Yes, it ____. | **Monday / is** | 多給的字卡：Sunday、isn't、does | 問「今天是星期一嗎？」用 Is it Monday today?，肯定回答是 Yes, it is. |
| Q04 | 拖放 | Jenny: Do you have ____ class on Monday?<br>Ken: Yes, I ____. | **art / do** | 多給的字卡：PE、does、don't | 圖中在畫畫，是 art（美術）課。用 Do you…? 問，就用 Yes, I do. 回答。 |
| Q05 | 拼字 | Tick turns a big page. A new day starts.（星期二） | **Tuesday** | 多給的字母：h、r、n | Tuesday（星期二）拼成 T-u-e-s-d-a-y，u 和 e 不要寫反。 |
| Q06 | 拖放 | Tick: Is it Monday today?<br>Ken: No, it ____. It's ____. | **isn't / Tuesday** | 多給的字卡：is、doesn't、Sunday | 今天不是星期一，要說 No, it isn't.，再補一句 It's Tuesday. |
| Q07 | 拼字 | Ken plays a recorder. Listen to the notes!（音樂） | **music** | 多給的字母：k、a | music（音樂）拼成 m-u-s-i-c，結尾是 c，不是 k。 |
| Q08 | 拖放 | Jenny: Do you have ____ class on Tuesday?<br>Ken: Yes, I ____. | **music / do** | 多給的字卡：art、does、is | 吹直笛是 music（音樂）課。用 Do you…? 問，就回答 Yes, I do. |
| Q09 | 拼字 | The blocks spin. A new day starts.（星期三） | **Wednesday** | 多給的字母：s、t | Wednesday（星期三）拼成 W-e-d-n-e-s-d-a-y，中間的 d 不發音，但要寫出來。 |
| Q10 | 拖放 | Tick: What ____ is today?<br>Ken: It's ____. | **day / Wednesday** | 多給的字卡：time、Monday、Tuesday | 問「今天星期幾？」用 What day is today?，回答 It's Wednesday. |
| Q11 | 拼字 | Look at the desk. What class is it?（數學） | **math** | 多給的字母：e、n | math（數學）拼成 m-a-t-h，結尾是 th。 |
| Q12 | 拖放 | Ken: ____ Jack have ____ class on Wednesday?<br>Jenny: Yes, he does. | **Does / math** | 多給的字卡：Do、Is、music | 主詞 Jack 是他（第三人稱單數），問句開頭用 Does。算盤代表 math（數學）。 |
| Q13 | 拼字 | Ken stands in the long hall. A new day starts.（星期四） | **Thursday** | 多給的字母：e、n | Thursday（星期四）拼成 T-h-u-r-s-d-a-y，開頭是 Th。 |
| Q14 | 拖放 | Tick: What day is ____?<br>Ken: It's ____. | **today / Thursday** | 多給的字卡：it、Tuesday、Wednesday | 問「今天星期幾？」用 What day is today?，圖上亮的是 Thursday（星期四）。 |
| Q15 | 拼字 | Look at the book on the desk.（國語） | **Chinese** | 多給的字母：a、s | Chinese（國語）拼成 C-h-i-n-e-s-e，開頭 C 要大寫。 |
| Q16 | 拖放 | Jenny: ____ you have ____ class on Thursday?<br>Ken: No, I don't. | **Do / Chinese** | 多給的字卡：Does、Is、math | 主詞是 you，問句開頭用 Do，不用 Does 或 Is。 |
| Q17 | 拼字 | Look at the headphones and the book.（英語） | **English** | 多給的字母：a、e | English（英語）拼成 E-n-g-l-i-s-h，開頭 E 要大寫。 |
| Q18 | 拖放 | Ken: Does he have ____ class on Thursday?<br>Jenny: Yes, he ____. | **English / does** | 多給的字卡：Chinese、do、is | 戴耳機看英文書是 English（英語）課。Does he…? 就用 Yes, he does. 回答。 |
| Q19 | 拼字 | Look at the microscope and the tubes.（自然） | **science** | 多給的字母：s、k | science（自然）拼成 s-c-i-e-n-c-e，開頭是 sc。 |
| Q20 | 拖放 | Ken: ____ Jenny have ____ class on Thursday?<br>Tom: Yes, she does. | **Does / science** | 多給的字卡：Do、Is、English | Jenny 是她（第三人稱單數），問句開頭用 Does。顯微鏡代表 science（自然）。 |
| Q21 | 拼字 | The school week is almost over.（星期五） | **Friday** | 多給的字母：e、r | Friday（星期五）拼成 F-r-i-d-a-y。 |
| Q22 | 拖放 | Ken: Does she have ____ class on Friday?<br>Tom: No, she ____. | **PE / doesn't** | 多給的字卡：art、don't、isn't | 體育館代表 PE（體育）。Does she…? 的否定回答是 No, she doesn't. |
| Q23 | 拼字 | Look at the globe and the map.（社會） | **social studies** | 多給的字母：e、n | social studies（社會）是兩個字：social＋studies。 |
| Q24 | 拖放 | Jenny: Do we have ____ class on Friday?<br>Ken: No, we ____. | **social studies / don't** | 多給的字卡：science、doesn't、aren't | 地球儀和地圖代表 social studies（社會）。Do we…? 的否定回答是 No, we don't. |
| Q25 | 拖放 | Tick: ____ it Sunday today?<br>Ken: No, it ____. | **Is / isn't** | 多給的字卡：Do、Does、don't | 問「今天是…嗎？」用 Is it…?，否定回答是 No, it isn't. |
| Q26 | 拼字 | Kids are playing in the park.（星期六） | **Saturday** | 多給的字母：n、e | Saturday（星期六）拼成 S-a-t-u-r-d-a-y。 |
| Q27 | 拖放 | Tick: Is it Monday today?<br>Ken: ____ | **Yes, it is.** | 多給的字卡：No, it isn't.、Yes, it does.、Yes, I do. | Is it…? 的肯定回答是 Yes, it is. |
| Q28 | 拖放 | Tick: Is it Monday today?<br>Ken: ____ | **No, it isn't. It's Tuesday.** | 多給的字卡：Yes, it is.、No, it isn't. It's Thursday.、No, I don't. It's Tuesday. | 今天不是星期一，是星期二，所以說 No, it isn't. It's Tuesday. |
| Q29 | 拖放 | Jenny: Do you have art class on Monday?<br>Ken: ____ | **Yes, I do.** | 多給的字卡：Yes, he does.、No, I don't.、Yes, it is. | Do you…? 的肯定回答是 Yes, I do. |
| Q30 | 拖放 | Jenny: Do you have Chinese class on Thursday?<br>Ken: ____ | **No, I don't.** | 多給的字卡：Yes, I do.、No, she doesn't.、No, it isn't. | Do you…? 的否定回答是 No, I don't. |
| Q31 | 拖放 | Ken: Does Tom have PE class on Tuesday?<br>Jenny: ____ | **Yes, he does.** | 多給的字卡：Yes, I do.、No, he doesn't.、Yes, she does. | Tom 是男生，所以用 he：Yes, he does. |
| Q32 | 拖放 | Tom: Does Jenny have music class on Friday?<br>Ken: ____ | **No, she doesn't.** | 多給的字卡：No, he doesn't.、Yes, she does.、No, I don't. | Jenny 是女生，所以用 she：No, she doesn't. |
