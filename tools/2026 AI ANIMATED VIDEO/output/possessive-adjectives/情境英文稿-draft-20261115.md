# 所有格情境動畫英文稿（v1，2026-11-15 老師改過第 1、5 段）

> 對象：國小六年級；每段 5 句；**粗體**＝要強調的所有格；最後一句都扣回 Free Time 句型。
> 角色暫定：Ken（I）、媽媽（you 對 Ken 說）、爸爸（he）、水鈴（she）、Ken＋水鈴（we）、爺爺奶奶（they）。

## 1. I → my（早上，Ken 的房間）
1. Ken: Good morning. I'm Ken.
2. Ken: This is **my** room.
3. Ken: This is **my** book.
4. Ken: And this is **my** baseball.
5. Ken: I play baseball in **my** free time.

## 2. you → your（玄關，媽媽對 Ken 說）
1. Mom: Ken, this is **your** water bottle.
2. Mom: Take more water.
3. Mom: And this is **your** cap. It's hot today.
4. Ken: Thanks, Mom!
5. Ken: I go jogging in **my** free time.

## 3. he → his（客廳，爸爸）
1. Ken: Look! This is my dad.
2. Ken: He likes music.
3. Ken: This is **his** guitar.
4. Ken: And that is **his** hat.
5. Ken: He plays the guitar in **his** free time.

## 4. she → her（水鈴的房間）
1. Shuiling: Hi! I'm Shuiling.
2. Ken: She is my friend. This is **her** room.
3. Ken: This is **her** cat, Mimi.
4. Ken: And this is **her** TV.
5. Ken: She watches TV in **her** free time.

## 5. we → our（學校籃球場，Ken＋水鈴）
1. Ken & Shuiling: We are in Class 601.
2. Ken: This is **our** classroom.
3. Shuiling: This is **our** teacher, Ms. Yeh.
4. Ken: And this is **our** basketball court.
5. Ken & Shuiling: We play basketball in **our** free time.

## 6. they → their（爺爺奶奶家）
1. Ken: They are my grandpa and grandma.
2. Ken: This is **their** house.
3. Ken: This is **their** dog, Lucky.
4. Ken: They love Lucky.
5. Ken: They walk **their** dog in **their** free time.

## 回顧（對回課本）
- What do you do in **your** free time? — I go jogging in **my** free time.
- What does she do in **her** free time? — She watches TV in **her** free time.
