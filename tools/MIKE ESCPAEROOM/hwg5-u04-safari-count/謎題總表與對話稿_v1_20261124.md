拖放挖空（照 World Routines，括號＝挖空）：
- 複數：How (many) ___ (are) there? There (are) N (animals). 干擾：much、is、單數字（例 koala）。
- 單數：How (many) ___ (are) there? There ('s) (one) (lion). 干擾：are、two、lions。
- 口說題目標句：There are three koalas.／There's one lion.（評分照 World Routines：數字、are／'s、複數 -s 都要對）。
- 拖放和口說交錯：每站一題拖放、一題口說。

## 三、六國對話稿

固定結構（每題）：
1. 國家主角開場（只在每站第一題）：Welcome to ___! Let's get on the ___.
2. 國家主角：Look! How many ___ are there?
3. 解謎 → 數圖 → I 版主角：There are N ___.／There's one ___.
4. 國家主角問 I 版主角的旅伴（traveler boy／girl，主角選女生時旅伴是男生）：**Does he like ___?** → I 版主角：**Yes, he does.**／**No, he doesn't. He likes ___.**
5. 答案動畫。

| 站 | 開場 | 第 1 題 | 第 2 題 | Does he/she like ___? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 台灣 Mei | Welcome to Taipei Zoo! Let's get on the zoo train. | How many pandas are there? → There's one panda. | How many bears are there? → There are two bears. | Does he like pandas? — Yes, he does. |
| 日本 Haruto | Welcome to Fuji Safari Park! Let's get on the jungle bus. | How many lions are there? → There are five lions. | （下車）Let's get off the bus and go to the farm. How many horses are there? → There are six horses. | Does he like lions? — No, he doesn't. He likes horses. |
| 肯亞 Amani | Welcome to Nairobi National Park! Let's get in the jeep. | How many zebras are there? → There are eight zebras. | How many rhinos are there? → There's one rhino. | Does he like zebras? — Yes, he does. |
| 印度 Arjun | Welcome to Ranthambore! Let's get in the jeep. | How many tigers are there? → There are two tigers. | How many monkeys are there? → There are seven monkeys. | Does he like tigers? — No, he doesn't. He likes monkeys. |
| 新加坡 Ling | Welcome to the Night Safari! Let's get on the tram. | How many elephants are there? → There are four elephants. | How many turtles are there? → There are nine turtles. | Does he like turtles? — Yes, he does. |
| 澳洲 Jack | Welcome to Australia! Let's get on the safari bus. | How many koalas are there? → There are three koalas. | How many lions are there? → There's one lion. | Does he like koalas? — Yes, he does. |

（主角選男生時，對話裡的 he 全部換成 she。）
